# Association of Legal Entities "Association Tatar and Bashkir national-cultural centerov Republic Kazakhstan"

## Contacts

- Executive director: Abdulkhalikov Rinat Abdulkhanovich

## About

The association united and represented some 250,000 Tatars and Bashkirs of Kazakhstan, coordinating the work of national-cultural centers across the country. Since 2016 the online radio station "Tatar Avazy" has been on air — a media project by Rinat Abdulkhalikov that preserves and promotes the work of Kazakhstani poets and writers who write in Tatar.

In 2015 Rinat Abdulkhalikov, as executive director of the Association of Tatars and Bashkirs of Kazakhstan, told The Astana Times that it included local centers from every region of the country and was one of the co-founders of the World Congress of Tatars. The association held language competitions, academic conferences on history and Sabantuy festivals, and its centers ran Sunday schools.

According to the World Congress of Tatars, the association of legal entities "Association of Tatars and Bashkirs of Kazakhstan" operated until 2016 alongside the "Idel" association. On 25 April 2017 a founding conference created the unified national public association "Kazakhstan Congress of Tatars and Bashkirs" (chaired by Taufik Karimov). No active website or social media of the association itself were found in 2026.
