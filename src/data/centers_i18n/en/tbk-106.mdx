## About

The Dimitrovgrad local Tatar national-cultural autonomy brings together the Tatars of Dimitrovgrad in Ulyanovsk Oblast. In January 2014 the autonomy marked its 15th anniversary with an exhibition and an evening festival of Tatar culture at the Voskhod house of culture, attended by a representative of the World Congress of Tatars. The current legal entity was registered on 30 January 2013; since June 2026 the autonomy's executive committee has been chaired by Azat Midarov.
