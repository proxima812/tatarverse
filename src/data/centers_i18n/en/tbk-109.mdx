# Public organization "Kievskoe society Tatars "Ildash" ("Sootechestvennik")

## Contacts

- Director: Khusnutdinov Kanafiya Misbakhetdinovich

## About

The "Ildash" ("Compatriot") society is part of the All-Ukrainian Tatar Cultural Center "Tugan Tel", which unites 13 regional Tatar societies of Ukraine; Kanafiya Khusnutdinov heads both organizations. The Tatars of Kyiv are a community with a long history, covered by the newspaper "Den".

According to Ukraine's Unified State Register, the public organization "Kyiv Society of Tatars 'Ildash' ('Compatriot')" was registered on 30 June 1993 (EDRPOU code 22907551) and is still listed as registered in 2026; its head is Kanafiya Khusnutdinov, and its legal address is the same as that of the "Tugan Tel" center. In 2021 the society was among the 12 Tatar organizations of Ukraine listed on the World Congress of Tatars website, but it no longer appears in the October 2026 edition of the directory. No website, social media or public contacts of the society could be found.
