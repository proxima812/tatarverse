## About

The autonomy is part of the Regional National-Cultural Autonomy of Tatars of Ryazan Oblast, formed at a founding conference in 2016 by the local autonomies of Ryazan, Kasimov and Sasovo.

Since 2023 the autonomy has been chaired by Farida Sabirova. Together with kindergartens, colleges and libraries it runs exhibitions of children's crafts and drawings, skullcap-painting workshops and a "Summer Offline" festival, introduces children to the poetry of Gabdulla Tukay and Musa Jalil, and holds the Tatar-language dictation "Tatarcha diktant" every year. Its founders include Faina Sitdikova, who set up the Nur public organization in Ryazan in the 1990s. The regional autonomy's website rnkat.ru is down as of October 2026: the domain no longer exists.
