## About

More than five thousand Tatars live in Sakhalin Oblast, mostly in Yuzhno-Sakhalinsk (about 1,230 people in the 2010 census), Okha, Kholmsk and Poronaysk. Alongside the city autonomy there are the Regional National-Cultural Autonomy of Tatars of Sakhalin Oblast (headed by Z. Z. Valitova), the Tatar educational and cultural center "Tugan Tel" (since 2006), a Sunday school and the national ensemble "Duslyk".

The city autonomy was registered as a legal entity on 30 July 2009; Vasilya Khuzina has been listed as its chair since October 2019 and remains so as of October 2026 (state register).
