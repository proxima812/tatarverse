## Contacts

- Director: Azat Galiullin (chair since October 2025 per the state register; previously headed by Ilgizar Zaripov)

## About

The autonomy unites the Tatars of Abdulinsky District, home to about 5,700 Tatars (2010), including more than 2,000 in Abdulino itself. The first mention of Abdulino (1795) is linked to Tatar settlers from Siberia. Tatar is taught as a subject in three schools of the district. The autonomy is part of the Orenburg regional Tatar national-cultural autonomy.

The autonomy was registered on 27 June 2005 and is listed among the national and cultural associations on the website of the Government of Orenburg Oblast. Azat Galiullin became its chair in October 2025.
