## About

Asekeevsky District is one of the areas of compact Tatar settlement in the Orenburg region: about 10,000 Tatars (2016) and 13 Tatar villages, and Tatars make up 80% of the population of the district center, Asekeevo. A branch of the Tatar Public Center operated here from 1993 and became a national-cultural autonomy in 1998. On the autonomy's initiative a park and a memorial to Musa Jalil were created in the district. Tatar is taught in four schools, and Tatar clubs run in two more schools and four kindergartens.

The current legal entity was registered on 5 August 2004; Eduard Gareyshin has chaired the autonomy's council since August 2019 (state register, as of October 2026).
