## About

The Buguruslan city public organization "Tatar Public Center Tugan Tel for the Revival of National Culture and Language" brought together the Tatars of Buguruslan in Orenburg Oblast. According to the list of national organizations kept by the Government of Orenburg Oblast, the center was founded in 1990 and worked at the Tukay house of culture; the legal entity was registered on 31 March 2003.

On 17 June 2021 the organization ceased to exist as a legal entity: its activity as a public association was terminated by a court decision under Article 29 of the federal law on public associations (state register). No active contact channels or signs of activity after that date were found.
