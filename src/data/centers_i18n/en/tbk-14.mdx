# Chastnyy Foundation "Mezhdunarodnye sozvezdiya Naziba Zhiganova"

## Contacts

- President: Tenisheva Svetlana Vladimirovna

## About

The foundation was set up in 2008 in West Kazakhstan Region (Uralsk) as part of the "World Names of Our Compatriots" initiative. Its founder is Svetlana Vladimirovna Tenisheva. The foundation aims to discover and support gifted children and to preserve the legacy of composer Nazib Gayazovich Zhiganov (1911–1988). It runs the International Children's and Youth Music Competition named after Nazib Zhiganov in Uralsk.

The foundation takes as its motto Zhiganov's view that a people's art stays alive only in dialogue with other cultures. Under its project "Mutual Enrichment and Interaction of the Musical Cultures of the Kazakh and Tatar Peoples" it researched archives in Almaty and Uralsk and reconstructed the biography of Gulzhaukhar Chumbalova, the first professional Kazakh female pianist and a musicologist of Abai's work; for her 105th anniversary it held lecture-concerts in Uralsk with artists of the regional philharmonic. Svetlana Tenisheva described this work as the foundation's president in Kazakhstanskaya Pravda in January 2025. The foundation is listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad (Uralsk). No website or social media of its own were found.
