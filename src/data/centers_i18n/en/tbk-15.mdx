## About

The Latvian Tatar-Bashkir Cultural Society "Chishme" was registered in Riga on 20 November 2000 and unites the Tatars and Bashkirs of Latvia; the state register lists it as active. The Tatar communities of Ventspils and Daugavpils joined it and are now formal branches with their own directors — Klim Suleymanov in Ventspils and Alim Ashirov in Daugavpils. The society belongs to the Ita Kozakeviča Association of National Cultural Societies of Latvia.

Its director, Zufar Zainullin, is the son of the Tatar writer Zaki Zainullin, a former military officer, a candidate of technical sciences and the founder of the "Tatarstan-Baltia" trading house; his Riga office serves as the society's premises for rehearsals and classes. In 2003 Zainullin was elected chair of the Consultative Council of Muslims of Latvia and later joined the Consultative Council of National Minorities under the President of Latvia. The deputy chair is Ildus Sabitov, and the "Chishme" ensemble is directed by Fauziya Bulusheva.

The society runs a vocal ensemble, a Sunday school and a library, and has published the quarterly bulletin "Chishme" since 2002. Young members of the ensemble perform at the annual children's folk art olympiad; the society holds cultural events, meetings and themed evenings and teaches the Tatar language to anyone interested, and it has organised concerts by Venera Ganeeva and Aidar Galimov. Together with the European Tatar Youth Association, the society initiated the first all-European Sabantuy, held in Riga's Vērmanes Garden on 7 June 2014 as part of the "Riga — European Capital of Culture" programme.

The last confirmed public activity was Nowruz in Riga on 24–25 March 2018. The society has no website or social media: the domain tatarlar.lv listed in the LNKBA directory no longer exists, and its former email address is unreachable along with the neonet.lv domain itself.
