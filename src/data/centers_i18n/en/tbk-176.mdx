## About

The "Shatlyk" ("Joy") association was founded in 1995 and marked its 25th anniversary in 2020. Since 1999 it has run the Gainan Kurmashev Sunday school in the building of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan of Aktobe Region, teaching the native language, literature, history, traditions and singing. A Sabantuy festival is held in Aktobe every year.

The Instagram page @shatlyk_aktobe describes itself in its profile as the Aktobe Oblast ethnocultural association of Tatars and Bashkirs "Shatlyk"; posts about Tatar language classes and events continue in 2026. The association is listed in the current World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad.
