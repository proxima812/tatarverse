## About

The Tatar cultural center "Tugan Tel" has been working in Taldykorgan since 1992; in June 2017 it celebrated its 25th anniversary at the regional Sabantuy. Chairman Khairulla Ibragimov said at the time that the association's board had 9 members, that about 2,500 Tatars live in Taldykorgan and 16,000 in the region, and that the center had branches in Tekeli, Sarkand and the village of Balpyk Bi. Taldykorgan is now the center of Zhetisu Region, and the current directory of the World Congress of Tatars lists the organization as the public association "Zhetisu Regional Tatar Cultural Center "Tugan Tel". The center's Instagram, @tuganteltdk ("Tugan Tel Tatar Culture Center of Zhetisu Region", about 950 followers), is active in 2026: the chairman posts addresses to followers, the center takes part in the "Mämle" Kazakh language club run by Qogamdyq Kelisim of Zhetisu Region, and in September 2026 it visited the Ilyas Zhansugurov Literary Museum. In April 2025 "Tugan Tel" held World Tatar Cuisine Day at the House of Friendship together with Qogamdyq Kelisim of Zhetisu Region.
