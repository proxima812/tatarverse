# Public Association "Ethnocultural Association of Tatars and Bashkirs of Shymkent Ak Bars"

## Contacts

- Director: Abdullin Ruslan Shavkatovich

## About

The "Ak Bars" association has been active since 2010. About 9,000 Tatars live in Shymkent. The association runs Sunday schools of the Tatar and Bashkir languages as well as dance, choir, art, sports and hiking clubs for city and regional residents of all ages, and holds national holidays, festivals, competitions and creative evenings. Its focus is on young people.

In November 2018, when Shymkent became a city of national significance, the association was chaired by Asya Mukhamedova, who had previously led the South Kazakhstan regional Tatar-Bashkir center; she said the city association would carry on the regional center's work (Sunday schools, clubs, national holidays) and add the autumn Chak-Chak Bairam festival. The association's emblem is a snow leopard holding a Greig's tulip. In the current World Congress of Tatars directory it is listed as the public association "Tatar Cultural Center "Ak Bars", Shymkent", separately from the public association "Tatar-Bashkir Ethnocultural Association of Shymkent". No website or social media of "Ak Bars" itself were found.
