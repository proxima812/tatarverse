# Public association "Kostanayskiy tataro-Bashkir national cultural center "Duslyk"

## Contacts

- Director: Bektimirov Rinat Valtaevich (member of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan)
- Address: 115 Altynsarin St, Kostanay

## Links

- [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/duslyk_kostanay/)

## About

The "Duslyk" Tatar-Bashkir ethnocultural center of Kostanay (BIN 950440002750) marked its 30th anniversary in 2025; it works at the House of Friendship of Kostanay Region. Later the center came to be headed by Tanzilya Khakimova. Its main festival is Sabantuy, which has become an event for the whole city (during the pandemic it was held online).

The Instagram page @duslyk_kostanay is run in the name of the "Duslyk" Tatar-Bashkir ethnocultural association and gives its address as 115 Altynsarin St; its latest posts date from August 2026, and its pinned stories cover Sabantuy 2024 and events for the 80th anniversary of Victory.
