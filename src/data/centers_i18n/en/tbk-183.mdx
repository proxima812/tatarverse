# Public association "Kyzylordinskiy Oblast Tatar cultural center"

## Contacts

- Director: Gizatullina Galiya Kamilovna

## About

The center began its work on 27 January 1991 and is the same age as Kazakhstan's independence. It was founded by Nagima Akhmadeeva; Galiya Gizatullina had been her right hand since the late 1990s and took over the center in 2003. Its work aims to strengthen interethnic harmony and to revive and preserve the native language, customs, historical memory and culture of the Tatars of Kyzylorda Region.

In 1991–1995 the center was chaired by Khabir Abdraimov and Garafetdin Gubaidullin; in the early years activists met on Sundays to sing, read Tatar literature and learn the native language. In 2013 the center marked Nagima Akhmadeeva's 95th birthday with the book "A Heart Given to People" and a concert, and later held a memorial evening for her centenary; in 2019 it proposed naming a Kyzylorda street after the builder Farit Fatkuldinov. The center is on the list of ethnocultural associations of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan in Kyzylorda Region and appears in the current World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad (2026).
