## About

The North Kazakhstan Regional Tatar-Bashkir Public Center "Duslyk" in Petropavlovsk preserves and promotes Tatar and Bashkir culture; it marked its 35th anniversary in 2024. The center is part of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan in the North Kazakhstan Region. It organizes Sabantuy on Petropavlovsk's central square, cultural and charity events, and takes part in the Day of Unity of the People of Kazakhstan and other regional ethnocultural events.
