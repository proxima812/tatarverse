# Public association "Tatar-Bashkir Ethnocultural association "Aynury" Zhambylskoy region"

## Contacts

- Director: Khalikov Amir Shafkatovich

## About

The "Ainury" association was founded in 1995 in Taraz (Jambyl Region); its first leader was Ekhat Khamatovich Safiulin. The association is home to the "Ainury" folk ensemble of the same name.

On the list of ethnocultural associations of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan in Jambyl Region the center appears as the public association "Tatar-Bashkir Ethnocultural Center "Ainury", chaired by Amir Khalikov. In December 2019 Khalikov, as chairman of the Tatar-Bashkir ethnocultural center, took part in a meeting of ethnocultural association leaders with the regional governor at the House of Friendship in Taraz. The association is listed in the current World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad (2026). No website or social media of "Ainury" were found.
