## About

The "Tatulyk" ("Harmony") association runs a choir, a dance group and a council of elders. It works to preserve and develop the culture of the Tatars and Bashkirs of Kazakhstan; Gulsina Baikenova speaks actively about the life of Kazakhstan's Tatars.

The association is based in Atyrau, at the House of Friendship, 129 Kulmanov Street. Its chair Gulsina Baikenova-Chukanbaeva is a ballet dancer who heads the "Ballet Atyrau" studio; in 2017 she was a delegate from Kazakhstan to the 6th Congress of the World Congress of Tatars. In October 2025 the House of Friendship hosted a celebration of the 30th anniversary of the Atyrau regional Tatar-Bashkir ethnocultural association and the 25th anniversary of the "Tatulyk" folk choir. The official Instagram @tatulyk_atyrau covers Tatar language lessons, national songs and dances and concerts, with posts in September 2026.
