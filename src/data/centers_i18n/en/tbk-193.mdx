## About

The Tatar Cultural Center of Uralsk works to preserve the identity, culture and traditions of the Tatars of West Kazakhstan Region. It runs a Tatar Sunday school, a council of elders, the "Yalkyn" folk group, the "Kүbәlәklәr" vocal group and the "Lәysәn" and "Yashlek" dance groups. The center looks after the Gabdulla Tukay museum, opened in 2006 in the house of Tukay's teacher M. Tukhvatullin: Uralsk is Tukay's "spiritual homeland", where the poet spent his childhood and youth.

The center's activists saved Uralsk's Red Mosque: its restoration was started by the center's then leader, entrepreneur Rishat Khairullin, and the mosque reopened in 2006. In November 2019 the center, with the support of the regional Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan, held an International Music Festival dedicated to Nazib Zhiganov. According to Kazakhstanskaya Pravda (September 2026), the 19th-century "Rakyibiya" madrasa building, restored with funding from Tatneft, will house the Tatar Cultural Center: the library has already moved there, Sunday school classes and "Yalkyn" rehearsals have resumed, and the official opening is planned for October 2026. The Instagram account @zhaek_tatarlary ("Tatar mәdәni үzәge | Tatar Cultural Center", the center's youth wing) is active in 2026.
