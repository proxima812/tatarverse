## About

The East Kazakhstan regional Tatar cultural center works in Ust-Kamenogorsk. After the Semipalatinsk and Ust-Kamenogorsk regions were merged in 1996, the center was re-registered. It operates alongside the Tatar organizations of Semey (the Tatar Public Center of Abai Region and the "Hak" association).

The center is listed in the current World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad (2026). The list of ethnocultural associations of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan for East Kazakhstan Region (undated) names Sergei Faizov as head of the Tatar ethnocultural association. No website, social media or public phone number of the organization could be found.
