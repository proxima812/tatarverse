## About

The association was registered on 28 July 2000 (BIN 000740005065). It does educational and cultural work to preserve Tatar culture and the native language in Almaty.

According to the state register (stat.gov.kz, egov.kz, via the uchet.kz service), the association's legal address is 111 Tattimbet St, Medeu District, and its head is Tursuntai Gabitov. The list of ethnocultural associations of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan for the city of Almaty includes an "Almaty Tatar Public and Cultural Center" chaired by Tursuntai Gabitov (85 Karasai Batyr St, House of Democracy, office 8), with Rinat Ayupov as deputy chairman. The organization is not in the current World Congress of Tatars directory (2026), where Almaty is represented by the Tatar-Bashkir Public and Cultural Center, the "Ildash" center and the Almaty branch of the Kazakhstan Congress of Tatars and Bashkirs. No website or social media of the association could be found.
