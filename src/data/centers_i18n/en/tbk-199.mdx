# Public association "Vostochno-Kazakhstanskiy Tatar public center"

## Contacts

- Chair of the board: Ismailova Kamilya Rashitovna

## About

The East Kazakhstan regional Tatar public center is based in Semey, the historical center of the Tatar community of the Irtysh region. It works together with other Tatar organizations of Semey (the "Hak" association and the Tatar School of Arts) and is part of the Kazakhstan Congress of Tatars and Bashkirs.

In 2022 Semey became part of the new Abai Region. The current World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad (2026) has no organization under this name: for Semey it lists the Tatar Public Center of Abai Region, the "Hak" association and the "Golden Cradle of the Turks" and "Irtysh Monnary" foundations, and for East Kazakhstan Region the Tatar National Cultural Center in Ust-Kamenogorsk. No website or social media of the center under its former name were found.
