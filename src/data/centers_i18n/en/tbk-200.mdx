# Public association "Yuzhno-Kazakhstanskiy Oblast tataro – Bashkir cultural center"

## Contacts

- Director: Mukhamedova Asya Irikovna

## About

The center operated in Shymkent, home to more than 10,000 Tatars and about 400 Bashkirs. In autumn 2018, after administrative changes made Shymkent a city of national significance, an initiative group proposed creating the "Tatar-Bashkir Ethnocultural Association of Shymkent" ("Ak Bars") on its basis. The center held Sabantuy festivals, including in Kentau.

By November 2018 Asya Mukhamedova, who had led the regional center, was already chairing the city association "Ak Bars" and said it would carry on the regional center's work: Sunday schools of the Tatar and Bashkir languages, clubs and national holidays. The center appears on the list of ethnocultural associations of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan in Turkistan Region, but not in the current World Congress of Tatars directory (2026), which lists the Turkistan Region branch of the Kazakhstan Congress of Tatars and Bashkirs for the area. No website or social media of the center were found; the site tatar-centre.ucoz.kz does not open (redirect loop).
