# Society for the Culture and Mutual Aid of Tatars of Konya Province

## Contacts

- Director: Rakhmetulla Kurtaran

## About

Konya is one of the centres of the Tatar diaspora in Turkey; Kazan Tatars from Konya take part in nationwide Tatar events, including the Sabantuy in Eskişehir. Turkey has more than 50 Tatar associations; descendants of settlers from Crimea and the Volga region have lived in Anatolia since the late 18th century.
