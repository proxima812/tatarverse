# Society for the Culture and Mutual Aid of Tatars of Kutahya Province

## Contacts

- Director: Atasever Abdullah

## About

Kütahya is one of the Turkish cities where Tatars settled. According to Tatar public figures in Turkey (Medyascope interview, 2025), the Tatar association in Kütahya has ceased operating; the main centres of Kazan Tatars in the country are Istanbul, Ankara and Eskişehir. Abdullah Atasever is listed as head of the Kütahya associations in the World Congress of Tatars directory.

The World Congress of Tatars directory also lists the organization as the "Society of Tatars of Kütahya" — it is the same body with the same head.
