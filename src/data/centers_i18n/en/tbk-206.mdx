# Society culture and mutual aid volzhsko-uralskikh Tatars Stambula

## Contacts

- Director: Gülten Urallı

## About

Istanbul, together with Ankara and Eskişehir, is one of the main centres of Kazan (Volga-Ural) Tatars in Turkey. The society traces its history to the mid-20th-century organizations of "Kazan Turks" (Kazan Türkleri Kültür ve Yardımlaşma Derneği), which published the magazine "Kazan" (from 1970). It cooperates with the Ankara society of Kazan Tatars (kazankultur.org).

The society's Turkish name is İdil Ural Türkleri Kültür ve Yardımlaşma Derneği, and its president is Gülten Urallı. Together with the plenipotentiary representation of Tatarstan in Turkey, the society holds the Sabantuy in Istanbul: in July 2018 the festival was opened by Urallı (QHA news agency). According to Tatar public figures in Turkey (Medyascope, 2025), the Idel-Ural association in Istanbul is still active. No website, social media or public contacts of the society could be found.
