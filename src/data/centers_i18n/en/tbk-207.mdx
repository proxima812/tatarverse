## About

The Tatar Culture Society of Estonia "Idel" is registered and operates in Tallinn. The Estonian commercial register contains a single legal entity under that name — MTÜ Eestimaa Tatarlaste Kultuuriühing "Idel", code 80100796, with an address in Tallinn; there is no separate "Idel" organisation in Tartu, and the Integration Foundation's official list of national minority cultural societies records no Tatar organisation in Tartu County at all. The city of Tartu entered this record from the directory of the World Congress of Tatars. The society has worked since 1995 and its director is Ilzija Ahmet. Members mark Muslim holidays, hold literary evenings in memory of Tukay and Cälil, and maintain a Sunday school of the Tatar language, a library and a Tatar choir; a Sabantuy gathering Tatars of the Baltic states is held every year.
