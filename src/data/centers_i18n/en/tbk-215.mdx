## About

The Tatar Community of Vilnius County was registered on 17 November 2004 and unites the Lithuanian Tatars of the city of Vilnius and the Vilnius district, coordinating the work of other Tatar organisations in the region. It emerged after the older Lithuanian Tatar Community of the Vilnius Region, which had branches in Nemėžis and the village of Forty Tatars, fell into decline. The community works in the House of National Communities at Raugyklos g. 25 and is not a member of the Union of Lithuanian Tatar Communities.

Since its foundation the community has been led by Galina Miškinienė, associate professor and doctor of humanities, an orientalist and Turkologist, senior researcher at the Institute of the Lithuanian Language and a descendant of the Lipka, the Polish-Lithuanian Tatars. She is the author of a catalogue of the Arabic-script manuscripts of the Lithuanian Tatars (2005) and the organiser of international conferences on the history and culture of Lithuanian Tatars at Vilnius University. In 2012 Miškinienė was elected the representative of Tatar societies in the Union of National Minorities of Lithuania, and became its chair in April 2013.
