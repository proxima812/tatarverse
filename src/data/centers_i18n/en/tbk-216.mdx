# Society of Tatar and Bashkir Cultures of Odesa and Odesa Oblast

## Contacts

- Director: Bulatov Rail Rafaelovich

## About

Tatars have lived in Odesa since the early 19th century — the city was multi-ethnic from its founding. The Society of Tatar and Bashkir Cultures of Odesa and Odesa Oblast is one of the organizations in the World Congress of Tatars directory.

According to the 1989 census, 3,747 Tatars and 551 Bashkirs lived in Odesa. A former chairman of the society, reserve colonel Mais Rakhimovich Battalov, was one of the initiators of the revival of Odesa's Muslim community: he secured its registration with the Odesa Regional Council, drafted its charter and later registered the "Religious Community of Muslims of Odesa" at the Al-Masar mosque ("Islam in Ukraine" portal, 2010). No website, social media or public contacts of the society could be found.
