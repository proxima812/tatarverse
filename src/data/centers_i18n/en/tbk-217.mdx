# Society Tatar culture "Agidel" Mykolaiv

## Contacts

- Director: Garipov Feliks Garipovich

## About

The Mykolaiv City Society of Tatar Culture "Agidel", led by Felix Garipov, is one of the national-cultural societies of Mykolaiv Oblast: it is listed in the information digest "Multinational Mykolaiv Region", published in 2012 by the regional administration's culture department and the regional children's library. According to the 2001 census, 1,255 Tatars lived in Mykolaiv Oblast. The society also appears in the current World Congress of Tatars directory of organizations abroad (October 2026). No website, social media or public contacts of the society could be found.
