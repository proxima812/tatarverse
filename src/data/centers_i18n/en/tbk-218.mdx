## About

The society is based in Kohtla-Järve, the centre of Ida-Viru County, where about five hundred of Estonia's more than two thousand Tatars live. The legal entity is MTÜ Ida-Virumaa Tatari Kultuuri Ühing, registration code 80054625, at Endla tn 4. Sources disagree on the year of foundation: the World Congress of Tatars gives 1995, the Estonian Wikipedia 1998.

On 12 May 2018 the society hosted the first All-Estonian Sabantuy in Kohtla-Järve. The following year its chair, Rinat Garifullin, headed the organising committee of the 4th All-European Sabantuy, which Estonia hosted in Tallinn.

The history of Estonia's Tatars goes back more than 600 years — central Tallinn has a district and a street named Tatari. The society has no website or social media: in the Integration Foundation's official list of national minority cultural societies its information-channels column is empty.
