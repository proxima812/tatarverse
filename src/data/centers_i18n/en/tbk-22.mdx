# Donetskaya City public organization "Society Tatar culture "Yoldyz"

## Contacts

- Director: Nadrshin Zakardya Vakhitovich

## About

The Donetsk City public organization "Society of Tatar Culture 'Yoldyz'" ("Star"), led by Zakardya Nadrshin, was still listed in 2021 among the 12 Tatar organizations of Ukraine on the World Congress of Tatars website; it no longer appears in the October 2026 edition of the directory.

Since 2014 Donetsk has been controlled by the unrecognized "Donetsk People's Republic", and since 2022 it has been annexed by Russia. According to World Congress of Tatars reports from 2024–2025, Tatar civic life in Donetsk is now represented by the Center for Tatar Culture in Donetsk (headed by Farida Hafizova) and the Donetsk Tatar national-cultural autonomy led by Rashid Bragin, chairman of the "DPR" Muslim Spiritual Administration; "Yoldyz" is not mentioned in these reports. No information about the society's activity after 2014 could be found.
