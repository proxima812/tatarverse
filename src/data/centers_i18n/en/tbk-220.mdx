## About

The union "Tatars in Belgium" (also called "Tatars of Belgium") is one of the most active Tatar communities in Europe. It is led by journalist Liliya Valeeva, a former press secretary of Tatarstan's Minister of Agriculture who moved to Brussels as a correspondent for "Selskaya Zhizn" and works at an international humanitarian foundation. In 2013 the "Alliance of Tatars of Europe" was founded at the European Parliament in Brussels with active participation of Belgian Tatars; Valeeva was elected secretary of its board.

The community holds Sabantuy, Tatar cuisine days and cooking master classes, Tatar culture days at children's camps, poetry evenings with Tatar poets, literary meetings and concerts, and cooperates with the Russian Center for Science and Culture.
