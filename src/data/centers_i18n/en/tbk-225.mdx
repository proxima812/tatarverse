- Director: Galiy Garipov

## About

The Vatandash ("Compatriot") center was founded in Pervouralsk in 2012 and has more than 20 members. It is part of the Congress of Tatars of Sverdlovsk Oblast ("Tatars of the Urals") and works with the representative offices of Tatarstan and Bashkortostan in Sverdlovsk Oblast. It supports Tatar and Bashkir amateur ensembles and organizes national festivals, including the Sabantuy.

In July 2024 Vatandash organized the city Sabantuy in Pervouralsk's Theatre Park with help from the municipal club system; among the guests of honor who addressed the audience was the center's council chair Galiy Garipov, a member of the executive committee of the World Congress of Tatars for Sverdlovsk Oblast. The organization is active, and its chair has not changed since registration in September 2012 (state register).
