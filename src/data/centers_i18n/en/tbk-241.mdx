## About

The Orenburg regional Tatar national-cultural autonomy was founded in December 1999 and re-registered in November 2009 (INN 5610008419). Since 10 July 2019 it has been chaired by Ildus Davlyatov, a member of the Legislative Assembly of Orenburg Oblast and a candidate of agricultural sciences. The Orenburg region is a historic center of Tatar culture and the birthplace of Musa Jalil; the autonomy unites the local Tatar autonomies of the oblast, including Abdulino, Asekeevo and Sol-Iletsk.

The autonomy's address is 4a Leninskaya St. in Orenburg, in the Yamashev library building, as listed both in the register of national organizations on the website of the Government of Orenburg Oblast and in the state register of legal entities (as of October 2026).
