## Contacts

- Director: Tagir Bikchantaev

## About

The regional Tatar national-cultural autonomy of Kemerovo Oblast was formed in 2015. Tagir Bikchantaev was born in the Tatar village of Sar-Saz in Yurginsky District and is the mufti of the Spiritual Administration of Muslims of Kuzbass. More than 40,000 Tatars live in the oblast (2010 census); local centers and autonomies work in Belovo, Berezovsky, Kemerovo, Novokuznetsk, Osinniki and Prokopyevsk. Tatarstan presented Kuzbass with a "Tatar farmstead" for the Sabantuy.

The autonomy was registered in Kemerovo on 16 December 2015; Tagir Bikchantaev remains chair of its board according to the state register as of October 2026.
