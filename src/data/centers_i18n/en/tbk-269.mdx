## About

The Union of Lithuanian Tatar Communities was founded in 1996; Romualdas Makaveckas, an associate professor at Kaunas University of Technology, was among those who started it. The Union unites twelve organisations: the communities of Alytus and Kaunas counties, the community of the Forty Tatars region, the Klaipėda society "Nur", the community of the city of Vilnius, the Panevėžys society, the communities of the Švenčionys and Trakai regions, VšĮ "Klaipėdos totoriai", VšĮ "Lukiškių kraštas", VšĮ "Pas Totorius" and the association "Tatar riders". The 2025 list of the Department of National Minorities gave eleven organisations and counted the Nemėžis community among them.

Since 1996 the Union has published the monthly "Lietuvos totoriai" in Lithuanian, Polish and Russian — the paper was founded in 1995 in Šalčininkai and has appeared in Kaunas since 1996. The Union maintains ties with the Leon Kryczyński Tatar culture centre in Gdańsk, the Belarusian association "Zikr al-Kitab" and the Mejlis of the Crimean Tatars.

According to the register of the House of National Communities, the Union is chaired by Motiejus Jakubauskas; it was previously led by Adas Jakubauskas, under whom Motiejus Jakubauskas served as deputy. Since 2018 a conflict has run within the community over the legitimacy of its leadership and over mosque construction projects.

Lithuania has sixteen registered Tatar non-governmental organisations in total; the 2021 census counted 2,142 Tatars living in the country.
