## About

Eskişehir Kazan Tatarları Kültür ve Yardımlaşma Derneği (Eskişehir Kazan Tatars Culture and Solidarity Association) was founded in 1992 in the historic Odunpazarı district by Kazan Tatars living in Eskişehir. Its goals are to strengthen solidarity among the city's Kazan Tatars, support students from Tatarstan and host visitors. In 2013 a Kazan Tatar museum house opened with the participation of the President of Tatarstan, showing a traditional Tatar interior, leather and metal crafts, musical instruments and old photographs of the settlers. Together with the Eskişehir municipality the association holds Tatar music concerts, including by the ethno group "Alpar".

The Tatars of Eskişehir are the largest Tatar community in Turkey. The association organizes the traditional Sabantuy in the Osmaniye quarter of the Çifteler district, held there for about 130 years; Kazan Tatars come from Antalya, İzmir and Konya, along with artists and Tatarstan's plenipotentiary representative in Turkey. The World Congress of Tatars directory lists the same association as the "Culture and Solidarity Association of Tatars of Eskişehir Province" (chair: Nadi Özdamar); in 2023, according to DHA, it was represented by Vedat Doğu. According to Tatar community figures in Turkey (Medyascope, 2025), Eskişehir has a single Tatar association.
