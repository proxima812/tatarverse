## About

The Stavropol regional public organization "Tatar Cultural Center Bayram" was registered on 3 October 2016 and has been chaired by Rustyam Khamitov since its founding (state register). Its registered address is in the aul of Maly Barkhanchak in Ipatovsky District, the main area of compact Tatar settlement in Stavropol Krai, home to about 900 Tatars in 2016. The village was founded in 1788 by settlers from the Vyatka and Kazan governorates. The village house of culture hosts the Tatar folklore groups "Alga" and "Chishmya" and a dance ensemble also called "Bayram" (since 2014).
