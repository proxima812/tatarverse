## About

Tatar club berlin ("Berlin | Tatarlar | Events") is the Instagram page of the Tatar children's club "Därtle chitek" in Berlin; as of October 2026 it has about 1,500 followers and 63 posts. The club is run by Läysän apa and describes itself on Telegram as a Tatar language and culture club for children. Its Telegram channel reposts videos from this Instagram account.

"Därtle chitek" opened in February 2024, with its first sessions held at Totschka Berlin on Prenzlauer Allee. Children learn the Tatar language, folk games, songs and dances, do handicrafts and prepare for Tatar holidays. The club holds "Karga botkasy" in Friedrichshain park and an open-air summer evening of Tatar songs, "Moñ kiche", and has taken its fairy-tale workshop to Tatar gatherings in Frankfurt am Main. In September 2026, together with the OKTO project, it launched a media course for teenagers aged 10 and over, taught by the journalist Elnar Baynazarov.

The page also promoted "Die Quelle | Chyganak", the first Tatar theatre project in Berlin. It was directed by Aidar Zabbarov and produced by Leysan Garaeva and Elza Nabiullina. Based on works by Ayaz Ishaki, the play was performed in Tatar with German translation on 13 and 14 March 2026 at Ballhaus Prinzenallee. Tickets were sold by the Berlin association Interkulturell-Aktiv e.V.
