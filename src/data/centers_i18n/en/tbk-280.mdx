## About

@tatar_ural_zko is the Instagram page of the public association "Ethnocultural Association "Jayek Tatarlary" of West Kazakhstan Region" in Uralsk: about 1,800 followers and more than 800 posts, active in 2026. The association is chaired by Flyura Milikeeva. In September 2026 the association and the Gabdulla Tukay Museum held the international research conference "Jayek Tatarlary" in Uralsk, marking the 140th anniversary of Tukay and the 115th anniversary of Nazib Zhiganov. The main program took place on 26 September at the House of Friendship with a book exhibition and a photo exhibition on the association's history, guests were welcomed by the "Yalkyn" folk group, and researchers from the G. Ibragimov Institute of Language, Literature and Art presented the Tukay museum with a six-volume edition of the poet's works. The program closed with a concert of Zhiganov's music at the G. Kurmangaliev Philharmonic.
