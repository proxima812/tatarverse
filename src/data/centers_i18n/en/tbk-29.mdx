## About

"Idel" is the Almaty branch of the national Association of Tatar and Tatar-Bashkir Public and Cultural Centers "Idel" (founded in 1989). On 14 November 2014 three centers — the Tatar city center "Idel" (1989), the Tatar-Bashkir center (1992) and the Almaty Center of Culture and Art (1996) — merged into the Tatar-Bashkir Public and Cultural Center of Almaty. It works in the same building and unites about 130 families (around 1,500 people).

The center occupies a whole wing of a two-story building: the city's largest library of Tatar literature, a workshop sewing national costumes, Tatar and Arabic language classes and an ensemble rehearsal hall. In 2015 Almaty entrepreneur Rashid Gizatullin bought the premises from a bank and gave them to the center free of charge. Almaty has held Sabantuy every year since 1989.
