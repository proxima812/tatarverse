# Tataro-Bashkir national center Balkhash

## Contacts

- Director: Galeev Damir Ibragimovich

## About

The Tatar-Bashkir National Center of Balkhash is a Tatar-Bashkir organization in Balkhash, Kazakhstan. It was listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad with Damir Ibragimovich Galeev as its leader.

No signs of current activity were found. The updated World Congress of Tatars directory of organizations abroad (2026) does not include Balkhash: for Karaganda Region it lists only the "Ikhlas" center and the Karaganda branch of the Kazakhstan Congress of Tatars and Bashkirs. The Balkhash center is also absent from the list of ethnocultural associations of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan in Karaganda Region. No website, social media or press coverage were found.
