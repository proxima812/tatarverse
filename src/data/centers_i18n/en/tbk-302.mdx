## About

The village of Keturiasdešimt Totorių (Forty Tatars) lies sixteen kilometres south-west of Vilnius, in the Vilnius district; the village's Tatar population today is a little over a hundred people. By tradition the first wooden mosque here was built in the reign of Grand Duke Vytautas; the present one was rebuilt in 1815, and beside it an old Tatar cemetery survives, protected as a cultural heritage site.

The village has a Tatar community house — one of three in Lithuania, alongside those in Nemėžis and Raižiai. It hosts holidays, exhibitions and educational programmes, and keeps the community's archive. The community belongs to the Union of Lithuanian Tatar Communities and offers guided tours and educational programmes on the sacred heritage of the Lithuanian Tatars by prior arrangement. Its chair, Fatima Buinovska, has been awarded the Order for Merits to Lithuania.

The community should not be confused with Keturiasdešimt Totorių gyventojų bendruomenė, which is the general residents' association of the village rather than a Tatar ethnic organisation.
