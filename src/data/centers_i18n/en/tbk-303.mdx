# Tatar community Izmir

## Contacts

- Director: Behzat Aktaş

## About

The Tatar community of İzmir is a Tatar organization listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of organizations abroad (in the current edition as the "Society of Tatars of İzmir"); Behzat Aktaş is named as its head. Kazan Tatars from İzmir take part in nationwide Tatar festivities: according to the DHA news agency, the 2023 Sabantuy in the Osmaniye neighbourhood (Çifteler, Eskişehir) drew guests from Antalya, İzmir and Konya. No website, social media pages or public contacts of the community could be found.
