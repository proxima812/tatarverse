## Contacts

- Director: Vahit Wafin, chair since 2014
- Address: Hämeenkatu 29 A 9, 33100 Tampere

## Details

- Registered by the Finnish Ministry of Justice on 10 April 1943
- Since 2019 the official name has been Tampereen tataariseurakunta ("the Tatar Congregation of Tampere"), previously Tampereen Islamilainen seurakunta
- 56 members according to official records for 2024; the historical maximum was about 150
- Its own prayer house on Hämeenkatu, bought in 1977, seats more than 100 people
- Tampere has a Tatar Muslim cemetery, one of three in the country

## About

This is a separately registered religious society rather than a branch of the Helsinki congregation: the Finnish Ministry of Justice entered it in the register on 10 April 1943. Until 2019 the congregation was called Tampereen Islamilainen seurakunta (in Swedish, Tammerfors islamitiska församling), and then changed its name to Tampereen tataariseurakunta, stating its Tatar character openly.

The first Tatars appeared in Tampere in the 1890s, drawn by the city's textile industry. The community bought the premises on Hämeenkatu in 1977 and finished renovating them by 1981; the prayer hall seats more than a hundred people. The first imam was Weli-Ahmed Hakim from Helsinki, and from 1947 the congregation was led by Habibur-Rahman Shakir, the best known of its imams. The city has a Tatar Muslim cemetery.

The congregation should not be confused with Tampereen Islamin Yhdyskunta, a different, non-Tatar Muslim organisation in Tampere.
