## About

The Lithuanian Tatar Community of the Vilnius Region had branches in Nemėžis and the village of Keturiasdešimt Totorių (Forty Tatars). After its activity faded, active members founded a separate Tatar Community of Vilnius County in 2004 — these are two different organisations with different chairs, not one under two names: in 2018 both appeared in the Lithuanian press as separate parties to the dispute over the legitimacy of the congress of Lithuanian Tatars and over mosque construction projects.

The community's chair, Aleksandras Beganskas, has also headed the board of the Council of Muslim Religious Communities of Lithuania (the muftiate) since 2019; he was re-elected to that post in December 2021.

The organisation's current state cannot be confirmed: it appears neither in the list of Tatar non-governmental organisations of Lithuania's Department of National Minorities nor in the current register of the House of National Communities. Open sources show no website, no social media and no public contacts for the community.
