## About

The Tatar Public Cultural Center of Yangiyul District is one of the regional Tatar associations of Uzbekistan and is listed in the directory of the World Congress of Tatars. For many years it was led by Anisa Adikhamovna Gataullina; after her death in the summer of 2025 her daughter, Alfira Nailevna Gilmanova, became chair (she was introduced to the members on 23 August 2025).

The center regularly holds a Sabantuy on the central alley of Yangiyul next to the town administration: in 2026 the festival took place on 24 May, attended by representatives of the Russian Embassy and of the Representative Office of Tatarstan in Uzbekistan. Together with the town's Russian, Korean and Kazakh cultural centers it takes part in the International Friendship Day, Navruz and Independence Day celebrations; it also hosts the "Chak-chak Bayram" harvest festival, memorial evenings and charity visits to people with disabilities and community elders. Since July 2025 the center has run the Telegram channel "Tatar Center of Yangiyul"; the latest posts date from October 2026 (the chair's participation in a national conference in Nukus on 29–30 September).
