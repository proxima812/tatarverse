# Tatar cultural center Alchevska

## Contacts

- Director: Karachurin Flyur Sayarovich

## About

The Alchevsk Tatar Cultural Center is a Tatar organization (Ukraine). It used to be listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad (in 2021 among the 12 Tatar organizations of Ukraine on the Congress website); it no longer appears in the October 2026 edition of the directory. The directory entry names Flyur Sayarovich Karachurin as its head.

After 2014, a local public organization, the "City Tatar Cultural Center 'Tan (Dawn)'", was registered in Alchevsk (Luhansk Oblast, controlled by the unrecognized "LPR" and, since 2022, under Russian jurisdiction) with the same head: it was registered on 27 August 2020 with Flyur Sayarovich Karachurin as chairman. According to the register, this organization was liquidated on 20 May 2026.
