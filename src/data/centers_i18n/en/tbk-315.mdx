## About

The "Bulgar" Tatar Cultural Center of Mangystau Region is a Tatar organization in Mangystau Region, Kazakhstan. It is listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad with Fandal Fattakhetdinovich Sakhibgareev as its leader.

The list of ethnocultural associations of the Assembly of the People of Kazakhstan in Mangystau Region includes the Tatar ethnocultural association "Bulgar" chaired by Fandal Sakhibgareev. In 2GIS, "Bulgar" is a Tatar ethnocultural association at the House of Friendship in Aktau (9, 34th microdistrict) linking to the Instagram account @tatary_aktau; that profile describes itself as "Tatar ethnocultural association Bulgar, Aktau", and its latest posts date from 2023. "Bulgar" holds Sabantuy festivals in Aktau: in 2023 together with the Bashkir center "Salavat", and on 7 June 2025 at the House of Friendship with guests from Tatarstan. The association has worked since the late 1980s and marked its 35th anniversary in 2024 with a concert of Tatar music and songs. Zulfiya Berneeva was previously listed as its leader.
