# Tatar cultural center "Chishme" in Krasnom Luche

## Contacts

- Director: Kabirova Gelfiya Zamilovna

## About

The "Chishme" Tatar Cultural Center in Krasnyi Luch is a Tatar organization (Ukraine). It used to be listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad (a 2021 study citing the Congress website lists it as the "Chishmә" center in Khrustalnyi); it no longer appears in the October 2026 edition of the directory. The directory entry names Gelfiya Zamilovna Kabirova as its head.

Since 2014 Krasnyi Luch has been controlled by the unrecognized "Luhansk People's Republic", and since 2022 it has been annexed by Russia; Ukraine has renamed the city Khrustalnyi. No current information about the center's activity after 2014 could be found — its fate is unknown.
