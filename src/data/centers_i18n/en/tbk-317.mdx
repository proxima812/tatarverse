## About

The organisation is registered as MTÜ Tatari Kogukond Eestis — "the Tatar Community of Estonia" — registration code 80107692, at Karjääri tn 4 in Maardu. That address is the municipal community centre Maardu Rahvamaja, not a private residence. In the directory of the World Congress of Tatars the organisation appears as the Tatar Cultural Centre of Maardu under the same director, Myadut Prokurorov.

The society traces its origins to Tatari Kultuuriselts, re-established in 1988 on the initiative of Timur Seifullen and renamed in 1995; the World Congress of Tatars gives 1990 as the year of foundation. The organisation took part in the Eesti Rahvuste Foorum and was one of the founders of Eestimaa Rahvuste Ühendus, and in the early 1990s its members helped re-establish Eesti Islami Kogudus.

The Tatar ensemble Kojash works at the Maardu community centre, which names support for the ensembles of national minorities, the Tatar one included, among its priorities. Muslim plots in Estonian cemeteries have been allocated in Maardu as well.

The society has no website or social media: in the Integration Foundation's official list of national minority cultural societies its information-channels column is empty.
