## About

The Tatar National Cultural Center of Fergana Region is one of the nine regional Tatar associations of Uzbekistan and is listed in the directory of the World Congress of Tatars. The center has been active since 2005; it was formerly led by Ravil Umarovich Tugushev, and in 2025–2026 its chair is Albina Ilyinichna Karimova. The "Kubalyak" ensemble performs under the center.

Every year the center holds a Sabantuy in Fergana (in 2025 and 2026 at the former Oil Workers' Palace), evenings in memory of Gabdulla Tukay (in April 2026 together with the philology department of Fergana State University) and the "Tatarcha Dictation" language event (November 2025). In April 2026 the chair, together with the deputy governor of Fergana Region, welcomed a delegation of cultural workers from Tatarstan in Oltiariq District. The center's phone number appears in its Sabantuy announcements for 2025 and 2026 and in the Golden Pages directory.
