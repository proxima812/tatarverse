## About

The Klaipeda Tatar society "Nur" belongs to the Union of Lithuanian Tatar Communities and is listed among the national communities of the city of Klaipeda at the municipal Centre of Ethnic Cultures. Klaipeda is one of the four largest Tatar communities in Lithuania, alongside Vilnius, Kaunas and Panevėžys.

Since 2014 the society has run the Sunday school "NUR", which teaches Tatar history, culture and customs as well as the Kazan Tatar language; attached to the school is the children's ensemble "Apipe", which performs songs and dances in the language of the Kazan Tatars. The society holds a city Sabantuy.

Klaipeda is also home to a separate legal entity, VšĮ "Klaipėdos totoriai", likewise a member of the Union of Lithuanian Tatar Communities. No website or social media of its own was found for the "Nur" society; the public point of entry is the page of the city's Centre of Ethnic Cultures.
