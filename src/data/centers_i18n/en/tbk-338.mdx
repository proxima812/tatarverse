## About

The Tatar Society of Visaginas was founded in 1997: Tatars came to this town to build Visaginas itself and the Ignalina nuclear power plant. The society is independent and does not belong to the Union of Lithuanian Tatar Communities. In 2022 it marked its 25th anniversary.

Since 1997 a Tatar Sunday school — the "Gimtosios mokyklos" study group — has run at the Visaginas culture centre, teaching Kazan Tatar language and literature, customs and rites, folklore, folk music, dance, traditional crafts and the history of the Lithuanian Tatars; the group is led by Zilya Karimova. Visaginas is one of five places in Lithuania with a Tatar Sunday school, alongside Kaunas, Klaipeda, Nemėžis and Vilnius. The society hosts the Tatar folk ensemble "Miras", directed by Irina Zakharenko, which plays Tatar folk instruments.

In 2018 the Visaginas Sabantuy was added to Lithuania's list of intangible cultural heritage, together with Tatar chak-chak.
