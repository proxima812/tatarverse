## About

The Tatar society "Miras" was founded in 2013. Based in Vienna, it brings together the Tatars of Austria. Its goal is to keep traditions alive and unite people who care about Tatar culture: the society regularly holds gatherings and organizes Sabantuy. Its ensembles, singers and dancers are regular participants in Austrian festivals and celebrations, where they perform Tatar folk songs and dances. The society is listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad; its director is Riva Rüdisser.
