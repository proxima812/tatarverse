## About

@tatary.aktau ("Tatars of Aktau") is an early Instagram account of the Tatar ethnocultural association "Bulgar" in Aktau: its posts carry the hashtag #татарскийцентрбулгар ("Bulgar Tatar center") and cover the center's Tatar Sunday school — alphabet lessons through games, handicrafts (valentines made of salt dough and felt), children's parties and tea with parents. The profile has 38 posts and about 120 followers; the last post is dated 5 February 2017, and the page has not been updated since. Later "Bulgar" ran the Instagram account @tatary_aktau, where in 2023 it posted announcements of Sabantuy, Chak-chak Bayram and performances of the "Kaspiy Dulkyny" vocal group.
