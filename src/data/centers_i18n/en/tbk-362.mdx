# Vsemirnaya Liga Tatars

## Contacts

- Director: Gönül Pultar

## About

The World League of Tatars is a Tatar organization in Istanbul listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad. According to BUSINESS Online (2019), the league began working in Turkey many years before the World Congress of Tatars was created, and its history goes back to the Tatar émigré public figure and writer Gali Akysh.

The league is headed by Gönül Pultar (born 1943 in Istanbul), a Turkish cultural studies scholar and novelist and the granddaughter of the politician and scholar Sadri Maksudi; English Wikipedia describes her as honorary president of the World League of Tatars. Pultar works on Tatar culture in the diaspora and in 2005 received a Russian medal for promoting it. No website or social media accounts of the league could be found.
