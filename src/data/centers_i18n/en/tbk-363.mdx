## About

The All-Ukrainian Tatar Cultural Center "Tugan Tel" ("Native Language") is an organization of Volga Tatars in Ukraine, active in Kyiv since 1998; it was entered in Ukraine's Unified State Register on 9 January 1998 and is still listed as registered in 2026. Its founder and president is Kanafiya Khusnutdinov, an Honoured Worker of Culture of Ukraine and Tatarstan. In 2005, with the support of Rustam Minnikhanov, the center opened the Tatar House in Kyiv, which housed a Sunday school (Tatar, Russian, Turkish and Arabic) and a library; since 2008 it has also hosted the Tatar-Bashkir youth center "Nur".

From March 2000 the center published the monthly magazine "Duslyk" ("Friendship") in a print run of 1,500 copies; the latest issue preserved in the web archive of the center's website is No. 5 of 2022. The center organized the All-Ukrainian Sabantuy and the Turkic-Tatar song contest "Kiev Sandugachy" ("Kyiv Nightingale"), and the vocal ensembles "Shatlyk" and "Talir" performed under its umbrella. According to World Congress of Tatars data cited in a 2021 study, 12 Tatar national-cultural organizations were registered in Ukraine.

On 31 May 2013 an extraordinary conference of the center voted no confidence in Khusnutdinov and appointed Ilmir Timergulov, head of the Crimean society of Volga Tatars "Idel", as president; the state register, however, still lists Khusnutdinov as the head of the organization. The center's website tatari-kiev.com is currently unavailable (checked in October 2026), so the contacts above may be out of date.
