## About

The Crimean Turks Culture and Mutual Aid Association (Kırım Türkleri Kültür ve Yardımlaşma Derneği) is the central organization of the Crimean Tatar diaspora in Turkey and the focus of its civic and cultural life. It was founded in Ankara on 26 June 1955 as "Kırım Türkleri Kültür Yardımlaşma ve Folklor Derneği" by Niyazi Kırımman, Server Trupçu, Mehmet Sevdiyar, Mahmut Oktay and İsa Karaşay. In 1990 it merged with other Crimean organizations and was restructured as the General Headquarters (Genel Merkez); in 1992 a Cabinet decision granted it public-benefit status.

Today the association has around 30 branches and representative offices, and has been led by Mükremin Şahin since 22 April 2017. The headquarters coordinates the aid campaigns that Crimean Tatar organizations run abroad and keeps close ties with communities in the USA, Germany, the Netherlands, Romania, Bulgaria and Australia. Its website is kept up to date: in September 2026 it reported on a conference, a "Crimea Day" at the Gordion Archaeofilm Festival and a meeting with Crimean Tatar refugees in Turkey.
