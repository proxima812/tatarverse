## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center "Temyasovo" is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan" in the village of Temyasovo, Baymaksky District. The center works to preserve and promote Bashkir history, language and traditional culture and organizes national events. Temyasovo is known as one of the historical capitals of Bashkir autonomy.

The center was established on 10 June 1994 by decree No. UP-264 of the President of Bashkortostan, which set up historical and cultural centers in Krasnokamsky and Baymaksky districts. It occupies 60 square meters in the Temyasovo rural multifunctional house of culture, which also hosts a branch of the G. Suleymanov children's art school and the village library. The village takes its name from Temyas Ilekeev, a tarkhan who took part in the Bashkir uprisings of 1737 and 1755. The center's VK group is updated regularly, with posts in October 2026.
