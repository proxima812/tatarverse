## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center "Saitbaba" is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan" in the village of Saitbaba, Gafuriysky District. The center preserves and develops Bashkir traditional culture, language and customs, and holds national festivals and educational events.

The center was established by decree No. UP-574 of the President of Bashkortostan of 29 September 2003 on creating historical and cultural centers of the peoples of the republic. It occupies two rooms with a total area of 67 square meters, an office and a museum room, in the two-storey building of the Saitbaba village administration, which also houses the village house of culture and a library. Saitbaba is one of the oldest villages in the district: according to the House of Friendship of Peoples, it was founded in 1704 and named after the landowner Saitbaba Kubekov, and in 1906 it had two mosques and two madrasas.
