## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center "Sarayly" named after Kanzafar-biy is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan" in Blagovarsky District. The center is dedicated to preserving the historical memory of the Bashkir clan and its leader Kanzafar-biy, and to developing the Bashkir language, traditions and culture.

The center was established by decree No. UP-65 of the President of Bashkortostan of 28 February 2011 and has been operating since 2012. It is based in the district palace of culture in Yazykovo, the district center. Kanzafar-biy led the Bashkir Min tribe and was one of the initiators of Bashkiria's voluntary accession to the Russian state: in 1554 he headed an embassy of Min elders to Kazan to ask for Russian allegiance.
