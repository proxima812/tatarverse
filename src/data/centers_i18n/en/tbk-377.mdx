## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center named after Musa Gareev is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan" in Ilishevsky District. The center bears the name of a local pilot, twice Hero of the Soviet Union Musa Gareev, and works to preserve Bashkir culture, language and historical memory and to foster patriotic education.

The center opened on 29 March 2019 under resolution No. 590 of the Government of Bashkortostan of 7 December 2018. It occupies two offices on the second floor of the M. Gareev cinema in Verkhneyarkeevo, the district center. Its VK group is kept up to date, with the latest posts in October 2026.
