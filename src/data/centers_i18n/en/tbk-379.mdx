## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Kurgan Oblast is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan", established in the village of Safakulevo. The center works with the Bashkir population of the Trans-Urals, preserving the language, traditions and customs and organizing national festivals and cultural and educational events.

The center was set up in 2020 by resolution No. 75 of the Government of Bashkortostan of 11 February 2020. Safakulevo was chosen because the district has the densest Bashkir population in the oblast. The center occupies four rooms with a total area of 66.3 square meters, two offices, a museum room and a storeroom, on the ground floor of a two-storey building. According to the House of Friendship of Peoples, the village itself was founded in 1782 by the family of Safa Yumagulov.
