## About

The Tatar Culture Society of Estonia "Idel" has worked in Tallinn since 1995 and remains the oldest Tatar association in the country. Its stated aim is to foster contact between the peoples of Estonia and the Tatar people and to promote the Tatar language and culture. Members mark Muslim holidays, hold literary evenings in memory of Gabdulla Tukay and Musa Cälil, and care for the Muslim cemetery. The society runs a Sunday school of the Tatar language and Arabic script, a library of Tatar literature and a Tatar choir.

In 2015 the society marked its 20th anniversary with a round table, "Tatars in multi-ethnic Estonia", which gathered every Tatar and Muslim organisation in the country. A Sabantuy is held every year, bringing together Tatars from across Europe; the society works closely with the other Tatar organisations of Estonia, in Narva, Kohtla-Järve and Maardu.

According to the commercial register, the society's board consists of Ilzija Ahmet and Favzia Hadiullina. The Integration Foundation lists Gulbstan Nugumanova as the contact person, and in 2018 the World Congress of Tatars called her the society's chair. The Estonian Wikipedia gives different data — 1996 as the year of foundation and Timur Saripov as chair — but these conflict with both the commercial register and the Integration Foundation's 2023 list.
