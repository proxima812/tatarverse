## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Orenburg Oblast is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan". The center was created as part of the decision to establish Bashkir historical and cultural centers in regions where Bashkirs live compactly, and works to preserve the Bashkir language, traditions and culture in the Orenburg area.

The center was established by resolution No. 75 of the Government of Bashkortostan of 11 February 2020 on Bashkir historical and cultural centers in other regions of Russia, and opened in Orenburg on 1 June 2020. It occupies a 52-square-meter office in a historic residential building dating from 1893. Its VK group, "Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Orenburg Region", is updated regularly, with the latest posts in October 2026.
