## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Chelyabinsk Oblast is tied to Argayash District, a historical center of compact Bashkir settlement in the Southern Urals (in the 1930s the Argayash Bashkir national okrug existed here). The center preserves and develops the Bashkir language, traditions and culture and organizes national festivals and educational work.

The center was established by resolution No. 75 of the Government of Bashkortostan of 11 February 2020 on Bashkir historical and cultural centers in other regions of Russia. According to the House of Friendship of Peoples, it works on the eighth floor of an office building in Chelyabinsk and occupies 70 square meters; the 2GIS directory still lists it in the village of Argayash. More than 120,000 Bashkirs live in Chelyabinsk Oblast, more than in any other region outside Bashkortostan.
