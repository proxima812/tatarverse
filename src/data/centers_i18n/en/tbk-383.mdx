## About

The Bashkir Historical and Cultural Center of Perm Krai is a branch of the state budgetary institution "House of Friendship of the Peoples of the Republic of Bashkortostan". The center was established to preserve the Bashkir language, traditions and culture in the areas of the Kama region where Bashkirs live compactly (including Barda District) and to organize national events.

The center was established by resolution No. 75 of the Government of Bashkortostan of 11 February 2020 and opened on 1 June 2020. It is housed in the Barda center of culture and leisure. The center works primarily with the Gayna (Tulva) Bashkirs, who founded more than forty settlements in the Tulva river basin. Its VK group is active, with the latest posts in September 2026.
