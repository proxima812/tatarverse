## About

The "Akbuzat" Bashkir Ethno-Cultural Center operates in Astana (Nur-Sultan) and is the second purely Bashkir center in Kazakhstan. It runs Sunday Bashkir language lessons by video link with teachers from Bashkortostan, the yiyin festival, Days of Bashkir Culture, workshops on playing the kurai and kubyz and on archery, as well as youth and summer programs. In July 2019 the center moved to the House of Friendship and joined the Assembly of People of Kazakhstan. At the World Kurultai of Bashkirs in Ufa (2019) it was recognized as the most active Bashkir center abroad.

The "Akbuzat" Bashkir ethnocultural association opened in the capital on 10 July 2017; its founders include Zakir Bikmukhametov and Ural Aznabaev, who had earlier led the first Bashkir ethnocultural center in Aktau. In 2020 it had about 40 activists, half of whom were learning Bashkir, and it was reviving traditional Bashkir archery. In August 2018 "Akbuzat" held the "Yiyin" folk festival in Astana's Central Park for the capital's 20th anniversary, with performers from Bashkortostan. Its Instagram @bashkir_astana ("BEKO Akbuzat") is active, with the latest posts in May 2026.
