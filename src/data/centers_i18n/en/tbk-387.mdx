## About

The Bashkir Cultural Center in Aktau is the first purely Bashkir national and cultural center in Kazakhstan, founded in 1990. It works to preserve the Bashkir language, culture and traditions among the Bashkir diaspora of Mangystau Region. Along with the "Akbuzat" center in Astana, it is one of the Bashkir associations of Kazakhstan, where Bashkir centers have historically operated together with Tatar ones.

The center is called "Salavat". According to The Astana Times, its first chair was Ural Aznabaev's father; Ural Aznabaev himself led the center in 2013–2015 and in 2017 co-founded "Akbuzat" in Astana. The center held Days of the Republic of Bashkortostan in Aktau (2013) and Sabantuy festivals: in May 2015 the Kaspiy stadium marked 25 years since the city's first Sabantuy (1990); in May 2023 the Sabantuy was held jointly with the Tatar association "Bulgar". The World Congress of Tatars directory lists the center as the "Salavat" society led by Usman Aznabaev. No website or social media of the center were found.
