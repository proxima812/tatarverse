## About

The center was registered in 1995. Its founder, Ismagil Lutfullovich Sharipov, led it for 18 years; in 2012 he was succeeded by Nuriya Niyazova. The center is based in the Medeu district of Almaty and has about 70 members. Charity is a major focus: since 2014 the center has run the "Road to School" drive at the Fatima mosque during Ramadan. One of its key goals is reviving the Fatima mosque and keeping it fully operational.
