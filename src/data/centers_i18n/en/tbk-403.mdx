## About

Almaniya Tatarca is a grassroots group of Tatars living in Germany. It organises Tatar-language events for children and adults, along with meet-ups and conversation clubs that help participants practise the language and stay connected to Tatar culture.

The group is one of the organisers of the Forum of European Tatars, scheduled for Frankfurt am Main on 25-27 September 2026. Elza Nabiullina represents Almaniya Tatarca as one of the German ambassadors of the Australian Tatar Forum - Kurultai.

The group is not tied to a single city: its meetings take place across Germany. Munich has a monthly conversation club at Bellevue di Monaco, Frankfurt has a book club and "Fatirnik" music evenings, and in Berlin the group announces trips and memorial gatherings. Its Telegram channel has 493 subscribers and is updated regularly; the latest post is dated 2 October 2026. The group runs the Forum of European Tatars together with the Frankfurt association Kultur- und Bildungsforum Quadrat e.V.
