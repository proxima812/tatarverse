# Tatars of Washington State

## Links

- [Website](https://tatarwa.org/)
- [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/seattletatars/)

## Highlights

- Founded and registered in January 2024
- Annual Sabantuy in Lynnwood: June 8, 2025 and June 7, 2026
- Not to be confused with "Tatar Society of Washington" (Washington, D.C.)

## About

Tatars of Washington State is a community of Tatars across Seattle, Bellevue and Lynnwood. In 2025 the state governor proclaimed June 8 as Sabantuy day, and the 2026 festival in Lynnwood drew hundreds of guests.
