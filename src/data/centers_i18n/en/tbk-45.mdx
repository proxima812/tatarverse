# Kirovogradskoe City society Tatars "Tugan il"

## Contacts

- Director: Abbyazov Oleg Kharisovich

## About

The Kirovohrad City Society of Tatars "Tugan Il" is a Tatar organization (Ukraine). It is listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad, which names Oleg Kharisovich Abbyazov as its head. Kirovohrad was renamed Kropyvnytskyi in 2016.

A 2021 study, citing the Congress website, calls it the "Kropyvnytskyi City Society of Tatars 'Tugan Il'"; it is still listed in the October 2026 edition of the directory. According to the collection "Ukraine — a Constellation of Cultures" (2003), quoted in a 2019 bibliography by the Kirovohrad Regional Research Library, 37 public organizations of national minorities were registered in the region, one of them Tatar. No website, social media or public contacts of the society could be found.
