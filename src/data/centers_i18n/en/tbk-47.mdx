## About

The Kokchetav Center of Tatar Culture was founded in April 1990, inspired by the writer and public figure Ibragim Nizamovich Salakhov; among its founders were Khakim Moldabaev, Gaziza Zakirova, Rauf Akberdin and other activists. Faruk Bogapov chaired it for many years; on Sabantuy day in 2012 leadership of the center (then the Public Association "Center of Tatar Culture of Kokshetau") passed to entrepreneur Mudaris Abdullin. The center held an annual December festival, "Min yaratam sine, Kokshetau" ("I love you, Kokshetau"), the "Sabantuy Yoldyzy" contest (since 2005), a Sunday school and the folk ensemble "Galiyabanu".

Today this line is continued by the Akmola Regional Tatar-Bashkir Ethnocultural Association, listed by the World Congress of Tatars as the "Association of Tatars and Bashkirs of Akmola Region". Since 2015 it has been led by Rafail Sulkarnaev, a graduate of the Tobolsk cultural college and the Chelyabinsk State Institute of Culture and former director of "Galiyabanu"; the association turned 30 in 2020. Its work covers native language classes, folk dance and singing, local TV programs, touring performances and the annual Sabantuy. The official Instagram @tataro_centr_akmola03 is active in 2026.
