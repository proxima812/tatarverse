## About

Medeni Tatarlar (@antalya.tatar) is an Instagram page of the Tatars living in Antalya. Its profile describes it as a source of news about the everyday life and creative work of the city's Tatars, above all members of the "Tatarstan" Culture Society (Tatarstan Mädäniyat Cämgiyate). As of October 2026 the page has about 1,800 followers and 240 posts. The "Tatarstan" Culture Society of Antalya is listed in the World Congress of Tatars' 2026 directory of Tatar organisations abroad.

The Tatar community of Antalya has been visible in the city since the late 2010s. In 2018 the city held its first Sabantuy, attended by more than 200 guests, and its first celebration of the Day of the Republic of Tatarstan. Both were organised together with the Tatar community by Oksana Nazarova, chair of the foreigners' council at the Konyaaltı district administration. In 2019 Enis Ziyalı, chairman of the Antalya Tatars Association, said that more than 2,000 Tatars from Tatarstan and other countries live in the city. Children are taught their mother tongue in a classroom at the Cemile Yusuf Delialioğlu Mosque in the Hurma neighbourhood of Konyaaltı; the community marks Gabdulla Tukay memorial days and hopes to open a Tatar school in the city.
