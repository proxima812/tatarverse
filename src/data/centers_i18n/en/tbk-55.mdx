# Cultural and educational society Tatars "Tugan il"

## Contacts

- Director: Kashaev Khamzya Khayrullaevich

## About

The Cultural and Educational Society of Tatars "Tugan Il" is a Tatar organization (Lviv, Ukraine). It was listed in the World Congress of Tatars directory of Tatar organizations abroad, which names Khamzya Khayrullaevich Kashaev as its head.

According to Ukraine's Unified State Register, its official name is the Lviv City public organization "Cultural and Educational Society of Tatars 'Tugan Il'" (EDRPOU code 19171750); it was registered on 24 May 2001 and is still listed as registered in 2026, headed by Khamzya Khayrullovych Kashaiev. A person with the same name and patronymic is listed as one of the co-founders of the public organization "All-Ukrainian Assembly of Tatars" (registered in Kyiv in 2015) with an address in Lviv. In 2021 the society was among the 12 Tatar organizations of Ukraine listed on the World Congress of Tatars website, but it no longer appears in the October 2026 edition of the directory. No website, social media or public contacts of the society could be found.
