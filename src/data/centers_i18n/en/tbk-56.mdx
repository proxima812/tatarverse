# Cultural and educational center "Tukay"

## Contacts

- Director: Altay Kurshat

## About

The Tukay Cultural and Educational Center is a Tatar organization in Turkey named after the poet Gabdulla Tukay; the directory entry gives no city and lists Altay Kürşat as its head. The current edition of the World Congress of Tatars directory of organizations abroad (checked in October 2026) no longer includes the center, and searches under Turkish, Tatar and Russian forms of the name turned up no website, social media or press coverage, so it is unknown whether the center is still active.
