## About

The society was founded in Riga in 2000; the Tatar communities of Ventspils and Daugavpils joined it. Its director, Zufar Zainullin, is the son of the Tatar writer Zaki Zainullin, a former military officer and a candidate of technical sciences; his office in Riga serves as the society's premises for rehearsals and classes. The quarterly bulletin "Chishme" has been published since 2002, and the popular "Chishme" ensemble works at the society. Zainullin was elected chair of the Consultative Council of Muslims of Latvia in 2003 and sits on the Consultative Council of National Minorities under the President of Latvia.

The Latvian state register contains a single entry for this society — Latvijas Tatāru-Baškīru kultūras biedrība "Čišma", registration number 40008055524, registered on 20 November 2000. Its former email address on the neonet.lv domain is unreachable: the domain itself no longer exists.
