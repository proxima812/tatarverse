## Contacts

- Director: Guzel Shaykhulova

## About

The local public organization "Center of Tatar Culture Kuray of the Asbest City District" was registered on 31 August 2015 in Asbest, Sverdlovsk Oblast, with Guzel Shaykhulova as chair of its council.

On 16 April 2020 the organization was liquidated by a court decision, and the termination was entered in the state register of legal entities. No active websites, social media groups or mentions of the center's work after the liquidation were found.
