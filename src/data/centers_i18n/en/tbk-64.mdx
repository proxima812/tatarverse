## About

The decision to create the autonomy was taken on 21 March 2005 at the third meeting of representatives of Tomsk's Tatar community. For many years it was led by Fail Kamalitov, a member of the Tomsk Oblast Legislative Duma who continued the work of his predecessor Murat Khusnutdinov; since May 2024 the chair has been Nail Mazitov. The autonomy works together with the Center of Tatar Culture, which has been operating for more than 25 years and holds about two hundred events a year with more than 15,000 visitors. The autonomy has sought the restoration of the historic building at 7 Moskovsky Trakt (estimated at about 30 million rubles) with support from the government of Tatarstan.

The autonomy's VK group is active, with posts in October 2026; in September 2026 Nail Mazitov represented Tomsk Oblast at a forum of Tatar national-cultural autonomies in Saint Petersburg.
