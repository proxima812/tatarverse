## Contacts

- Director: Rafik Kerimov

## About

The organization is based in the village of Ust-Uza in Shemysheysky District, founded by Tatar service people at the end of the 17th century and still the center of Tatar culture in the district. Ust-Uza hosts communal iftars and national festivals with the participation of the Tatar National-Cultural Autonomy of Penza Oblast.

The society was registered on 19 May 2003 with its legal address in Ust-Uza; Rafik Kerimov remains its chair according to the state register as of October 2026.
