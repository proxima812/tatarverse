## About

"Yolduz" ("Star") is the sports club of Finnish Tatars, founded in Helsinki in 1945 and a member of the Finnish sports union SVUL. The club is based in the five-storey building of the Finnish Islamic Congregation on Fredrikinkatu and runs sports days, fitness events and summer and winter camps all year round.

Tatar sport in Finland was preceded by the football and ice hockey club Altın Orda ("Golden Horde"), founded in 1933 in Terijoki — a separate and earlier organisation. The pride of "Yolduz" is its Tatar football team, which plays in the Helsinki division league and in friendly matches against teams of other minorities in Finland; some players have been invited to well-known Finnish clubs. For decades the club has gone on skiing camps in Pajulahti together with the Jewish sports club Makkabi and plays football against it.

The club is active: the chair of the congregation described it as part of the community's current life in 2020. The club has no website or social media of its own and is represented on the congregation's channels.
