## About

Nemėžis (Nemež) is a small town 8 km from Vilnius and the spiritual centre of Lithuania's Muslims: Grand Duke Vytautas settled Tatars here in 1397. The first wooden mosque was built in 1684 and the present one in 1909; under Soviet rule it served as a warehouse, and it was returned to the community and restored in 1993. It is one of Lithuania's four working mosques, alongside those in Kaunas, the village of Forty Tatars and Raižiai; Lithuania is the only Baltic country where a mosque can be visited.

Nemėžis has a Tatar community house — one of three in Lithuania, together with the village of Forty Tatars and Raižiai. It hosts holidays, exhibitions and educational programmes and keeps the archive. In 2020 the community founded a Sunday school, with the children's folk ensemble "Efsane" attached to it.

The community preserves the culinary heritage of the Lithuanian Tatars — chebureki, šimtalapis, šurpa, kolduny and pilaf — and takes part in the European Union project "Atrask krašto šaknis iš naujo" on developing cultural tourism. Since 2019 a separate legal entity, the association "Totoriškas skonis", has worked at the community under the same Tairas Kuznecovas: it presents 620 years of Lithuanian Tatar cooking at Nowruz, Sabantuy, exhibitions and fairs.

The community's former website on the domain nemeztatarcom.webs.com, still listed on the Vilnius District Municipality's site, no longer exists.
