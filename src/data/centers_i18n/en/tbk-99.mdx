## About

The association was officially registered in Riga on 24 October 2011 under the Latvian name Eiropas tatāru jaunatnes apvienība; the Latvian state register lists it as active. It initially comprised representatives of the five most active European countries — Latvia, Estonia, Lithuania, the Czech Republic and Germany; Tatar youth from England, Finland and Poland joined later.

The organisation holds forums of European Tatar youth: the first took place in Riga on 27–29 April 2012 and gathered more than seventy delegates from ten European countries and five regions of Russia, the second in Tallinn on 23–25 August 2013, and the third in Berlin on 29 September – 1 October 2017. Together with the "Chishme" society the association co-organised the first all-European Sabantuy, held in Riga on 7 June 2014 as part of the "Riga — European Capital of Culture" programme. The association also takes part in Nowruz celebrations in Riga and cooperates with the Alliance of Tatars of Europe.

The last confirmed public activity was Nowruz in Riga on 24–25 March 2018, where Oleg Sharipov spoke as the association's chair. No website or social media was found for the organisation; the domains of the Alliance of Tatars of Europe no longer exist either.
