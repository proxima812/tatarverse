The site publishes reference information about Tatar, Bashkir, Tatar-Bashkir and Crimean Tatar cultural centers, communities, associations and public initiatives in Russia and abroad.

## 1. Editorial policy

### The goal of the project

The project helps people find a cultural or community organization near them: a diaspora group, a national society, a youth association, language classes.

The site is a reference directory, not an official register of organizations.

### Where the information comes from

Listings are based on open data:

- official websites of organizations;
- their pages on social networks and messengers;
- publications and reference materials;
- information organizations publish about themselves;
- submissions sent through the form on this site.

More on the [Sources](/en/sources) page.

### Submissions and moderation

Any visitor can add a center or correct a listing: through the [Add a center](/en/submit) form or the edit button on a center's page.

A submission is not published right away. A moderator checks it against open sources, clarifies details if needed, and only then approves or rejects it. The logo and the map location are set by the moderator.

An approved edit appears on the site immediately, without waiting for the next update. You can track your submissions under [My submissions](/en/submissions).

### Corrections

Found a mistake in a name, address, link or description? Send an edit from the center's page. It is checked the same way as a new submission.

External links break or move over time. We fix them as we find them.

## 2. Privacy

### Browsing the site

You don't need an account to browse the site, and it doesn't ask for your name, phone number or address.

Standard technical data may be processed when you visit: server log entries and information about your browser, device and operating system. It is used to load pages and keep the site running.

### What is kept in your browser

Some data stays on your device, in your browser's local storage, and is never sent anywhere:

- your language and theme;
- saved centers (the [Saved](/en/saved) page);
- access keys for your submissions, which power My submissions, editing and withdrawing a submission;
- your submitter details from the form, so you don't have to type them again.

Clearing the site data in your browser removes all of this. The server shows a submission key only once, so after clearing you won't be able to manage older submissions from this device.

### What is sent with a submission

The form sends data to the catalog server at `api.tatarverse.cc`:

- the center details you filled in;
- your comment, if any;
- optionally, your name, role and contacts (phone, Telegram or email). These fields are not required;
- your IP address and browser information, to protect against spam and abuse.

Submitter contacts let a moderator ask follow-up questions. They are never published on the site and are visible only to moderators with a separate access right.

To filter out automated submissions, the form uses Google reCAPTCHA. The script loads only on the form page and is covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### Erasing submitter data

You can ask us to erase your name, contacts, IP address and browser information from a submission: write to us on [Telegram](https://t.me/mirikhan1). The submission itself and the center's edit history are kept.

### Telegram Mini App

The site can be opened inside Telegram through the [@tatarverse_bot](https://t.me/tatarverse_bot) bot. It is the same site: it does not get your phone number from Telegram and does not store your account data.

### What the site does not do

- it does not sell personal data;
- it does not use it for advertising or mailings;
- it does not require personal data to view pages.

## 3. External links

The site links to many third-party resources. Once you follow a link, that resource's rules apply. The site is not responsible for their content.

## 4. Content and responsibility

The information on the site is for reference. We try to keep listings accurate and up to date, but we can't guarantee that none of them contain mistakes or outdated details. Double-check anything important through the organization's official channels.
