The catalog collects information about Tatar, Bashkir, Tatar-Bashkir and Crimean Tatar cultural centers, communities, national organizations and public initiatives.

Listings are compiled from open sources and from submissions sent by the communities themselves and by visitors. A moderator checks every submission.

> The information on this site is for reference.

## Primary sources

- [Tatar Congress](https://tatar-congress.org);
- official websites of organizations and national-cultural autonomies;
- organizations' pages on social networks and messengers;
- open publications online;
- submissions through the [Add a center](/en/submit) form and edits from center pages, sent by organization representatives, community members and visitors.

## Secondary sources

To check and refine the information we also use:

- websites of friendship houses, cultural centers and public associations;
- regional and municipal websites;
- publications by cultural and community projects;
- maps and directory services, to check addresses, cities and the spelling of names;
- public pages on Instagram, Telegram, VK and other social networks.

## How the sources are used

- Each listing has a primary source and, where needed, additional ones; they are shown on the center's page.
- A moderator checks submissions against open sources before publishing them.
- When sources disagree, we choose the more cautious wording.
- Listing texts follow a common structure so the catalog is easy to read and filter.

## Limitations

- The site is not an official government or legal register of organizations.
- Information on third-party resources may have changed since it was added to the catalog.
- Some listings stay incomplete until someone fills them in.

> Being listed in the catalog does not confirm an organization's current legal status.

## Corrections and clarifications

Spotted a mistake or outdated details? Press the edit button on the center's page and send a correction through the form. If a center is missing, add it via [Add a center](/en/submit). You can follow your submission under [My submissions](/en/submissions).

To get an edit accepted faster, include a source: the official website, a community page or a recent publication.
