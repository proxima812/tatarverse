The site has three language versions: Russian, English and Tatar.

All three cover the menus, filters, buttons, labels, service pages, the submission form and My submissions. All blog publications are also translated into Tatar.

> **A translated interface does not mean every listing is translated.**

## Center listings

Listings live in the catalog's shared database, and each field can have a Russian and an English version. The English center page uses the English text when there is one.

Names and locations are translated for most centers, while the short description and the About section are translated only for some so far. When there is no translation, the English page shows the name and contacts and leaves the description out rather than falling back to Russian text.

A translation appears on the site as soon as a moderator approves it.

Listings are not translated into Tatar. On the Tatar version, the catalog shows the listings' Russian data, and links to centers open the Russian listing pages.

## How to help with translation

The submission form takes text in the language of the page: on the English version of the site, an edit is saved as the listing's English text. On the Tatar version it is saved as Russian text, since listings have no Tatar text. To add or fix a translation, open the center in the English version and send an edit from its page. A moderator checks it like any other submission.

## Available languages

- **Русский** - the main language of the interface and listings
- **English** - the interface, service pages, the submission form and some center descriptions
- **Татарча** - the interface, service pages, the submission form and all blog publications; center listings are in Russian

Other languages will be added once their interface translation is ready.
